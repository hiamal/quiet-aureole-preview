Womb Medicine Wheel

A 12-Week Sacred Feminine Embodiment Journey. Turn the wheel from Earth to Fire, Air and Water — through integration, and out into the Cosmic Womb.

12 weeks · OnlinePrice TBC · Coming 2027
Join the waitlist → See the curriculum

The invitation

The wheel turns through earth, fire, air and water — and so do you. Come back into your body, your desire and your intuition.

Womb Medicine Wheel is a 12-week sacred feminine embodiment journey with Giada Lovelight. It works through Earth, Fire, Air, Water, Integration and the Cosmic Womb, with themes of grounding, sensuality, desire, creativity, sexuality, clarity, intuition, emotional feeling, receptivity, embodiment and integration.

Giada guides women in Wollongong and Cronulla, and online wherever you are. This course is held online.

The medicine wheel

Four elements, one wheel.

Each element is a doorway into a different part of you. Together they form a wheel you can return to for the rest of your life.

  • Element one

    Earth

    The body, the ground beneath you, and the grounding to begin.

  • Element two

    Fire

    Sensuality, desire and creative life force.

  • Element three

    Air

    Clarity, breath and intuition.

  • Element four

    Water

    Emotional feeling, receptivity and flow.

The 12-week curriculum

Turning the wheel.

Twelve weeks, moving Earth → Fire → Air → Water → Integration → Cosmic Womb. Each week you receive guidance on what to study and practise.

  1. Stage one

    Earth

    Arrive in your body. Begin with grounding — the steady foundation every other part of the journey rests on.

    • Grounding
  2. Stage two

    Fire

    Wake up your life force. Explore sensuality, desire, creativity and sexuality as sources of vitality, not shame.

    • Sensuality
    • Desire
    • Creativity
    • Sexuality
  3. Stage three

    Air

    Clear the mind and open to inner knowing — listening to what your body has been trying to tell you.

    • Clarity
    • Intuition
  4. Stage four

    Water

    Let yourself feel. Move with your emotions, and practise the art of receiving.

    • Emotional feeling
    • Receptivity
  5. Stage five

    Integration

    Gather the four elements into one embodied whole, and bring them into daily life.

    • Embodiment
    • Integration
  6. Stage six

    Cosmic Womb

    The wheel opens out. Rest in the Cosmic Womb — spacious, creative and held.

The week-by-week schedule will be shared with the waitlist as the first guided journey is confirmed.

How the course works

Self-paced, and guided.

Womb Medicine Wheel is designed as a self-paced online course, so it fits around your life — without weekly live teaching. Each week you receive guidance on what to study and practise.

  • Self-paced online learning
  • Weekly guidance on what to study and practise
  • Course material, recordings and resources
  • The monthly online Women's Moon Circle, complimentary for eligible clients

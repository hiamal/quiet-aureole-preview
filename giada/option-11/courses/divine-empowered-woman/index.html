Divine & Empowered Woman

A 12-Week Womb Awakening Journey. Walk the feminine archetypes, map your womb and meet the elements — and be initiated into your own Divine and Empowered Woman.

12 weeks · OnlinePrice TBC · Coming 2027
Join the waitlist → See the curriculum

The invitation

Maiden, Mother, Lover, Crone — you carry all of her. This is the journey of meeting each one, and coming home to your womb.

Divine & Empowered Woman is a 12-week womb awakening journey, guided by Giada Lovelight. It moves through the feminine archetypes, into Divine Feminine embodiment, Womb Mapping and the elements, and arrives at an initiation into your own power.

Giada guides women in Wollongong and Cronulla, and online wherever you are. This course is held online.

The 12-week curriculum

The arc of the journey.

Twelve weeks, moving from the archetypes, through the body and the elements, to the stars — and finally, initiation. Each week you receive guidance on what to study and practise.

  1. Movement one · The archetypes

    Maiden → Mother/Queen → Lover → Crone

    The journey begins with the feminine archetypes. Meet each phase of woman within you — the parts you have honoured, the parts you have avoided and the parts waiting to be reclaimed.

    • Maiden
    • Mother / Queen
    • Lover
    • Crone
  2. Movement two · Into the body

    Divine Feminine embodiment & Womb Mapping

    From story to sensation. Drop into Divine Feminine embodiment, and map your womb — listening to what she holds, what she has carried and what she is ready to release.

    • Divine Feminine embodiment
    • Womb Mapping
  3. Movement three · The elements

    Earth, Fire, Air & Water

    Work with the four elements as living medicine, each opening a different doorway into your body, your desire and your voice.

    • Earth
    • Fire
    • Air
    • Water
  4. Movement four · Beyond the self

    Cosmic Womb & the Triple Goddess

    Widen out to the Cosmic Womb and the Triple Goddess — seeing your own cycles as part of something far larger.

    • Cosmic Womb
    • Triple Goddess
  5. Movement five · Arrival

    Divine & Empowered Woman initiation

    The journey closes with an initiation into your own Divine and Empowered Woman — a ceremonial crossing from who you were into who you are becoming.

The week-by-week schedule will be shared with the waitlist as the first guided journey is confirmed.

How the course works

Self-paced, and guided.

Divine & Empowered Woman is designed as a self-paced online course, so it fits around your life — without weekly live teaching. You are never left alone with it: each week you receive guidance on what to study and practise.

  • Self-paced online learning
  • Weekly guidance on what to study and practise
  • Course modules
  • Workbooks for reflection and integration
  • Pre-recorded guided journeys
  • The monthly online Women's Moon Circle, complimentary for eligible clients

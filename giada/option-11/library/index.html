The idea

A quiet shelf of practices to carry home — for the days between sessions, and the days you cannot get to one.

Giada is preparing the Shine Your Light Library as a home for guided meditations, breathwork recordings, audio journeys, digital workbooks, mini courses and other online resources.

It sits at the gentlest end of the journey, alongside 1:1 sessions in Wollongong and Cronulla, private programs and online courses.

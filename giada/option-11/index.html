Who this is for

You have done the work, held it together and kept everyone else afloat. Somewhere along the way you stopped feeling. This is where you come back home.

Giada works with women, men and couples who feel disconnected from their bodies, their desire or each other — and are ready to feel again.

Sessions are held in person in Wollongong and Cronulla, and online wherever you are.

  • You feel numb, flat or stuck on autopilot
  • You live in your head and want to come back into your body
  • Old beliefs, anxiety or low self-worth keep running the show
  • Intimacy, sensuality or desire feel distant or complicated
  • Your relationship needs safety, honesty and a new way to connect
  • You are ready to meet your shadow with compassion, not shame

The transformation

From numb to alive.

This work meets you in the body, not just the mind. Through tantra and embodiment, hypnotherapy, energy healing and honest conversation, Giada helps you soften what has been guarded and wake up what has been asleep.

  1. From numb to presentFeel your body, your breath and your emotions again.
  2. From shame to self-loveBefriend the parts of you that you learned to hide.
  3. From guarded to connectedOpen to intimacy, with yourself and with the people you love.
  4. From surviving to shiningLive from your True Self, with energy, pleasure and purpose.

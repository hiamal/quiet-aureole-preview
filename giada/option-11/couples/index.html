Couples & Relationships

Conscious Couples & Intimacy

Relationship and intimacy sessions for couples: communication, emotional safety, nervous-system regulation and connection. In Wollongong, Cronulla and online.

The goal

The goal isn't simply to stop arguing. It's to learn how to meet each other differently.

A compassionate, safe and non-judgemental space for two people to slow down, feel safe and reconnect, with each other and with themselves.

What we explore

Nine ways to reconnect

Every session is shaped around the two of you. These are the threads we may draw on.

  1. Conscious relationship coachingPractical, compassionate guidance for the relationship you want to build together.
  2. CommunicationLearning to speak and listen in ways that bring you closer.
  3. Emotional safetyA space where both of you can be honest without fear of judgement.
  4. Relationship patternsGently uncovering the emotional patterns, limiting beliefs and nervous system responses that keep you stuck.
  5. Nervous-system regulationHelping both bodies move out of fight, flight or freeze so you can meet each other with more calm.
  6. BoundariesHealthy boundaries that protect connection rather than create distance.
  7. IntimacyCloseness that is emotional as well as physical.
  8. Sensual connectionReawakening presence, touch and desire at a pace you both feel good about.
  9. Tantra-informed practicesPresence-based practices that deepen connection. Tantra & Embodiment

Is this for us?

When you want to meet differently

Couples come for many reasons. You might recognise yourselves in one of these:

  • You keep having the same argument in different words.
  • One or both of you feel disconnected, guarded or shut down.
  • Intimacy and desire have drifted and you'd like to find your way back.
  • You're moving through a transition, heartbreak or grief together.
  • You want to deepen what is already good between you.

My approach is gentle, trauma-aware and deeply respectful of your individual journey, and personalised rather than a standard protocol.

Welcome

You weren't meant to spend your life simply coping. You were meant to feel calm, confident, deeply connected and fully alive.

If you're feeling anxious, overwhelmed, emotionally exhausted or disconnected from yourself, know that lasting change is possible.

My story

Helping you find her again

Many of the women I work with have spent years putting everyone else's needs before their own. They arrive feeling burnt out, struggling with self-worth, navigating relationship challenges, heartbreak, grief or major life transitions. Deep inside, they know they've lost connection with the woman they truly are.

My role is to help you find her again.

I'm Giada Lovelight, founder of Shine Your Light Temple, offering personalised transformational sessions in Wollongong, Cronulla, and online.

Every session is uniquely designed around you.

Whether you're seeking support for anxiety, burnout, low self-worth, relationship challenges or simply wanting to feel more like yourself again, you'll be welcomed into a compassionate, safe and non-judgemental space where genuine transformation can unfold.

Giada's approach

A compassionate, safe and non-judgemental space

Many of the women Giada works with have spent years putting everyone else's needs before their own. They arrive burnt out, struggling with self-worth, or navigating relationship challenges, heartbreak, grief or major life transitions.

Rather than following a fixed approach, Giada integrates the therapies that will best support your growth, which may include holistic counselling, hypnotherapy, breathwork, nervous system regulation, NLP, EFT, shadow work, Reiki and sound healing.

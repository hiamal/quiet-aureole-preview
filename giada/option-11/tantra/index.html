Why this work

Many women grew up learning to disconnect from their bodies. Today, we have the opportunity to reconnect.

To silence their inner wisdom and place everyone else's needs before their own. This work is a gentle way back: to your body, your feminine essence and the woman you have always been.

The work

Sensuality as presence, not performance

In my work, Tantra-informed practice is a path back into the body: breath, awareness, sensation and feeling, met with presence rather than pressure. It sits alongside the somatic practices, breathwork and nervous system regulation that run through all of my sessions.

The language is mature and the pace is yours. My approach is gentle, trauma-aware and deeply respectful of your individual journey. Rather than following a standard protocol, I create a personalised experience that honours your story.

If you have questions about what a session involves, you are warmly welcome to ask before you book.

The invitation

Ongoing community for women on the path — without the weight of a weekly call.

The Women's Moon Circle is a monthly online gathering held by Giada Lovelight. It is ongoing community and integration for women enrolled in her programs and courses, so the courses never depend on weekly live calls.

Join from Wollongong, Cronulla or anywhere in the world.

Inside the circle

What we do together.

Each circle can include a blend of these practices, held with warmth, safety and care.

  1. ReflectionPause and notice where you are, this moon.
  2. IntegrationLet what you are learning settle into your life.
  3. MeditationGuided stillness to return to yourself.
  4. BreathworkMove energy and soften the nervous system.
  5. EmbodimentCome back into your body, through movement and feeling.
  6. SharingBe heard, and hear other women.
  7. ConnectionSisterhood that continues between circles.

Timing & access

Around the moon.

The circle is monthly. It does not need to fall on the Full Moon — timing can work around the New Moon, the Full Moon, or between the moons.

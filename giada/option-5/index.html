About Giada

Why me?

I have a strong connection to Mother Earth, the natural elements and the Womb of Creation.

I help women and men to find their own truth, their blockages and traumas, hidden in their minds, bodies and wombs, so they can rise up and ignite their own power, creativity, feel love from within and flourish.

I specialize in preparing women for a healthy and lovely pregnancy and to birth their baby naturally. I work intuitively and serve you through energy work and my tailored empowering programs. I honour every client as a unique and authentic being.

If you feel lost or confused I can guide you to clarity, and help you set success-driven goals so you can shine your light brighter.

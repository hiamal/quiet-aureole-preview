The approach

Conscious and subconscious

Susanne employs an integrated approach that blends strategic psychotherapy with clinical hypnotherapy, tailoring her interventions to meet each client's unique needs and goals. She begins by collaboratively setting clear, achievable goals to ensure the therapy process is guided by the client's aspirations for change.

Drawing on her extensive training in systems theory and cognitive-behavioral techniques, Susanne analyzes the underlying patterns contributing to the client's challenges. With a deep understanding of the interplay between thoughts, emotions, and behaviors, she designs targeted interventions to break free from unproductive cycles.

Once these patterns are identified, Susanne seamlessly incorporates clinical hypnotherapy to access the client's subconscious mind, facilitating profound exploration and transformation. Through guided hypnosis, she helps clients reframe limiting beliefs, alleviate deep-seated anxieties, and foster positive behavioral changes.

By combining direct guidance, skill-building exercises, and exploratory conversations with hypnotherapy techniques, Susanne creates a powerful synergy that addresses both conscious and subconscious aspects of the client's experience. This comprehensive approach fosters meaningful shifts, empowering clients to overcome emotional blocks, create positive change, and achieve lasting transformations in their lives and relationships.

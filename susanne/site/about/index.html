Susanne's story

iHer path

My life's path has been a testament to the incredible power of transformation. I've walked through the depths of a challenging 17-year-long abusive relationship, emerging from that darkness as a beacon of hope and healing for others. It was during this tumultuous period that my life's purpose crystallized: to guide individuals toward their better selves and facilitate profound positive changes in their lives.

iiHer practice

In my diverse and extensive background, I wear multiple hats—as a Clinic Hypnotherapist and Strategic Psychotherapist, a Law of Attraction and Empowerment Coach, a Meditation Teacher, and a Master Quantum Healer. My educational qualifications include a Masters of Metaphysical Science, grounding me in solid metaphysical principles.

Having personally battled the debilitating effects of anxiety, fear, and traumatic memories, I'm deeply committed to helping others overcome these emotional challenges. I empower my clients to see themselves in a positive light, a crucial step towards healing and personal growth.

iiiHealing

Over three decades, my healing journey has unfolded, allowing me to refine my skills as a gifted Healer and Medical Intuitive. My abilities as a Psychic Medium enable me to connect with the spiritual realm, offering guidance and insights to those in need.

What sets me apart is my deep understanding of quantum processes. I've leveraged this knowledge to craft powerful techniques that foster deep healing and transformation, targeting emotional and physical pain while releasing toxins within the body.

Explore the Quantum Processes

ivApproach

My holistic approach taps into the principles of quantum energy, aiming to restore balance and harmony in both body and mind. I've developed specialized techniques that particularly focus on aligning and cleansing the body's chakras, those vital energy centres crucial for overall well-being.

Through my guidance, I empower individuals to access their innate healing capacities, embarking on a journey toward renewed vitality, emotional clarity, and spiritual growth. My mastery of these quantum processes opens the door to a new level of healing and self-discovery for those who seek my guidance.

vHope

My life story stands as an inspiration, a testament to the incredible potential for transformation and renewal. It's my earnest desire to offer hope and healing to all who seek my expertise and support.

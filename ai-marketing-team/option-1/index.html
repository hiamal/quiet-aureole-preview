I. The problem & the promise

Marketing is a job. You already have one.

You know marketing matters. You also know the posts go out late, the messages wait, and the offer goes quiet whenever real work gets busy.

The problem

A real marketing team is slow and costly to hire. Learning every AI tool yourself becomes another full-time job. So the work stays half-done — or it stays with you.

The promise

One AI marketing team, built around your business and handed over fully set up. No tool-stitching, no prompt engineering, no half-built system left on your desk. You open it and use it.

II. What’s included

Seven AI team members, one brief.

  1. i.

    Strategist

    Turns your offer and audience into a plan: what to say, to whom, and where. Briefs the rest of the team.

  2. ii.

    Copywriter

    Writes posts, emails, ads and page copy in your voice, from the strategist’s brief.

  3. iii.

    Video copywriter

    Scripts short-form video — the hook, the beats, the captions and the call to action.

  4. iv.

    Publisher

    Formats finished content for each channel you use and gets it out on schedule.

  5. v.

    SEO & AEO

    Shapes your content so search engines and AI answer engines can find it and understand it.

  6. vi.

    Conversations & DMs

    Picks up comments and direct messages, answers the common questions, and points warm leads to your next step.

  7. vii.

    Analyst

    Watches what’s landing, reports in plain English, and tells the strategist what to do next.

Each is an AI team member with a clear job. Together they work as one team, set up around your business.

III. How it works

We build it. We hand it over. You use it.

1

We build it

We set up the whole team around your offer, your audience and your voice — the roles, the tools and the connections between them.

2

We hand it over

You receive a team that is fully set up and walked through with you. Nothing half-built for you to finish.

3

You use it

Brief the team, look over what it makes, and get back to serving your clients.

The point of “done for you” is simple: the setup is our job, the using is yours.

IV. Who it’s for

For people who sell expertise.

  • The coach with an offer and no time to market it.
  • The consultant whose best clients come from showing up consistently.
  • The course creator who wants content and conversations handled.
  • The small business owner who would rather not become a marketer.

An honest note

This isn’t a button that runs your business while you’re away. It’s a team that works for you, in your voice, on your offer. You stay the owner of the business and the decisions.

VI. Questions

Asked often.

What exactly is an AI marketing team?

A set of AI team members, each with one marketing job — strategy, copy, video scripts, publishing, search, conversations and analysis — set up to work together around your business.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We do the building and the wiring. You’re handed a team that’s set up, with a walkthrough on how to use it.

What do I actually do after handover?

You use it: brief the team, look over what it produces, and decide what goes out under your name. The routine work is what the team is there to take off you.

Will it sound like me?

That’s the goal of the setup. The team is built around your offer, audience and voice. You stay in the loop to keep it on-brand.

What does it cost?

We don’t publish a price here. Apply or book a call and we’ll talk through what’s involved and whether it’s a fit.

I’d rather learn to build it myself. Can I?

Yes — that’s what the Build Your AI Team workshop and the AI Business Challenge are for. See the three ways in above.

02 — what’s included

Seven AI team members. One team.

01

Strategist

Turns your offer and audience into a plan: what to say, to whom, and where. Briefs the rest of the team.

02

Copywriter

Writes posts, emails, ads and page copy in your voice, from the strategist’s brief.

03

Video copywriter

Scripts short-form video: the hook, the beats, the captions and the call to action.

04

Publisher

Formats finished content for each channel you use and gets it out on schedule.

05

SEO & AEO

Shapes your content so search engines and AI answer engines can find it and understand it.

06

Conversations & DMs

Picks up comments and direct messages, answers the common questions, and points warm leads to your next step.

07

Analyst

Watches what’s landing, reports in plain English, and tells the strategist what to do next.

…each one an AI team member, set up around your business and working as one team.

06 — faq

Questions, answered.

What exactly is an AI marketing team?

A set of AI team members, each with one marketing job — strategy, copy, video scripts, publishing, search, conversations and analysis — set up to work together around your business.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We do the building and the wiring. You’re handed a team that’s set up, with a walkthrough on how to use it.

What do I actually do after handover?

You use it: brief the team, look over what it produces, and decide what goes out under your name.

Will it sound like me?

That’s the goal of the setup. The team is built around your offer, audience and voice. You stay in the loop to keep it on-brand.

What does it cost?

We don’t publish a price here. Apply or book a call and we’ll talk through what’s involved and whether it’s a fit.

I’d rather learn to build it myself. Can I?

Yes — that’s what the Build Your AI Team workshop and the AI Business Challenge are for.

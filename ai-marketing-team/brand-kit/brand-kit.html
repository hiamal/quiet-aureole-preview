IX · Motion

Watch time, not app time.

Motion is slow and mechanical. The dial turns once every few minutes; hands step with weight; hovers settle on a long ease-out. All of it collapses under prefers-reduced-motion.

XI · Icons

Drawn in hairline.

Icons are instruments: circles, ticks and hands drawn in 1–1.6px strokes. Every example here is lifted from the live page.

Do

  • Stroke in #D9B56B, #8F6B2E or #C6B892; hands in #F3E2B0.
  • Build from circles, ticks and straight hands on a square grid.
  • Use round line caps; keep strokes between 1px and 1.6px.
  • Use the Cinzel → glyph as the only arrow.

Don’t

  • Use heavy filled or two-tone icon sets.
  • Fill shapes — except tiny 0.55rem status dots.
  • Add gradients, shadows or rounded “app” containers.
  • Mix in emoji or pictograms.

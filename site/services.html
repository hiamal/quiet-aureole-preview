Strategic Psychotherapy

Susanne employs a dynamic and focused approach to strategic psychotherapy, tailoring her interventions to each client's unique needs and circumstances. She begins by collaboratively setting clear and achievable goals with her clients, ensuring that the therapy process is guided by their aspirations for change. Drawing from her extensive training in systems theory and cognitive-behavioural techniques, Susanne keenly analyzes the underlying patterns contributing to the client's challenges. With a deep understanding of the interplay between thoughts, emotions, and behaviours, she strategically designs interventions aimed at breaking free from unproductive cycles. Susanne is adept at pinpointing critical junctures for change and skilfully guiding her clients through practical steps to address and overcome their concerns.

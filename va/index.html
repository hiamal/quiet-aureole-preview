What VS is & why it matters

What VS is

Example tasks · 12 Steps

What VAs are already doing for other clients

These are proven examples — not a fixed list. DeBorah can allocate any task. Work examples in order the first time, then as daily checklists. Full SOPs live in the markdown playbook source.

01

Friends pipeline

Joining FB Groups

Groups are your friend pipeline. Cold Add Friend from nowhere looks spammy and gets ignored. Members of relevant groups already share interest — warmer adds, higher acceptance, cleaner chats later. Goal: join approved niche groups, answer membership questions on-brand, and build a steady pool for Step 02 without join-spam.

02

Like + comment → higher acceptance

Add Friend

Bare Add Friend = low acceptance + spam signals. Like + comment on their 1st and 4th post raises recognition and acceptance — they see a real human who noticed them, not a lead-gen bot. Goal: send requests that get accepted, warm the relationship before Chat Sequence 1, and pace adds to protect the account.

03

20 min pre + 20 min post engagement

Posting FB Post

Facebook’s algorithm watches what happens around a post. Posting into silence = dead reach. Twenty minutes of engagement before and twenty after signals the post is alive — more distribution, more eyes, more DMs later. Goal: publish on-brand posts that get reach and feed the content engine behind chats, groups, and events.

04

New Friend → Facebook Group

Chat Sequence 1

A new friend who sits silent is wasted reach. Sequence 1 moves them from accepted Friend → Facebook Group — the community layer where trust and Free Event invites land better. Goal: open a warm Messenger thread after accept, guide them into the client’s FB Group, and log status in the Tracker.

05

FB Group member → Free Event

Chat Sequence 2

The Free Event is the conversion bridge. Group members already opted into community — Sequence 2 invites them to show up (webinar / live / challenge). More event participants = more bookings downstream for Coach’s Tech clients. Goal: move members into Free Event registration/attendance, keep tone invitational, and keep the Tracker honest.

06

Warm openers from friend list

Daily Birthday Messages

Birthdays are a permissionless warm open. People expect messages that day — perfect to restart dead threads or start new rapport without feeling salesy. One of the easiest ways to keep the friend list productive. Goal: message today’s birthdays, open conversations naturally, and soft-route warm replies toward Group / Free Event when appropriate.

07

Comment / re-comment for algorithm

Past Post Boost

Old posts die unless you poke them. A fresh comment or re-comment can resurface content in feeds, stretch lifespan, and keep the page looking active between new posts. Goal: revive past posts per Boss quota, spark new replies/likes on aging content, and support page health alongside new posting.

08

Anti-spam hygiene

Cancel Sent Friend Requests

Unaccepted friend requests sitting for days/weeks are a spam signal. Facebook watches pending volume. Cancelling stale requests is account hygiene — same league as not blasting adds without engagement. Rule: cancel unaccepted friend requests; don’t let them rot. Goal: clear stale pending requests and protect Add Friend capacity.

09

Emojis, titles, BGM, captions

Beautify Text & Video Posts

Ugly posts get scrolled past. Clean formatting, smart emojis, and polished video (BGM, captions, title, studio sound) raise watch time and comments — which feeds the algorithm and the DM engine. Goal: ship scannable on-brand text and video ready for Step 03’s 20/20 engagement window.

10

Invite button · ~30/day

Invite Friends into FB Group

The Group is the community hub. Inviting friends via the Invite button grows membership without relying only on DMs. Capped volume (~30/day) protects the account while still compounding growth. Goal: send ~30 Group invites per day (or Boss-set cap), prioritise warm friends, and feed Welcome Video Tagging and Sequence 2.

11

Tag new members → more event joins

Welcome Video Tagging

New members who never see the Welcome Video stay cold. Tagging them on the Welcome Video notifies them, pulls attention, and lifts Free Event participation — a high-leverage Group habit. Goal: tag new members on the approved Welcome Video, drive views + event curiosity, and hand warmer leads into Sequence 2 / registration.

12

Conversation progress → dashboard stats

Add Data to Coach’s Tech System Tracker

If it’s not in the Tracker, it didn’t happen for leadership. Conversation progress in Facebook only becomes dashboard stats when you log it in the Coach’s Tech System Tracker. Amal and clients steer from those numbers — messy logging = bad decisions. Goal: update the tracker from real progress, keep dashboard stats accurate, and close every shift with clean data.

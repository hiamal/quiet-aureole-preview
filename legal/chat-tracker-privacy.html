Privacy Policy — Chat Tracker by CoachTech

Effective: 6 October 2026 · Publisher: CoachTech / AI Marketing Suite · Contact: support via your CoachTech account

Chat Tracker (“the Extension”) helps coaches insert message templates into social messaging sites and sync outreach activity to their CoachTech (AI Marketing Suite) workspace and connected CRM.

1. Data we process

  • Account data you provide when signing in (email, name, session token) via CoachTech authentication.
  • Message templates from your Chat Map boards (titles and body text) loaded from CoachTech servers.
  • Outreach logs when tracking is enabled: destination profile identifiers/names visible on the page, platform, URL, and which template was used — sent to CoachTech APIs to update your CRM/chat progress.
  • Local preferences stored in Chrome storage (e.g. last selected board, tags, pipeline id).

2. How we use data

Data is used only to provide Chat Tracker features: template insertion, DNC/tag checks, CRM contact updates, and Chat Progress pipeline updates for your own workspace. We do not sell personal data.

3. Where data is stored

Extension preferences stay in your browser (chrome.storage). Account, boards, and send logs are stored on CoachTech production systems (api.coachstech.com / your workspace database) under your account controls.

4. Third parties

The Extension runs on third-party sites (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Telegram, Bluesky, TikTok, Reddit) only to read page context needed for templating/tracking and to insert text you choose. CRM sync uses your connected CoachTech CRM integration. We do not use advertising trackers in the Extension.

5. Permissions

Browser permissions (storage, tabs, scripting, webNavigation, and host access listed in the Chrome Web Store listing) are used solely for the Extension’s stated purpose. Host access to social sites enables content scripts; host access to CoachTech domains enables login, board sync, and tracking APIs.

6. Retention & deletion

Remove the Extension to clear local storage. Delete or export workspace data via your CoachTech account / support. CRM records follow your CRM retention settings.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions: contact CoachTech support through your AI Marketing Suite / CoachTech account, or the publisher email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
